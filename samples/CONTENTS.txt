Synthetic Connect IQ fixtures (no personal ride/location data)
============================================================
terrain-course.fit: 2 km straight-line course, 10 m records, flat elevation.
terrain-activity.fit: matching GPS/distance stream, 1 Hz, speed 10 m/s,
200 seconds. Power cycles 180 W / 220 W / 260 W every 10 seconds;
seconds 190-199 contain genuine zero power.

Course points:
  500 m   S 210-230
  700 m   Down 6%
 1000 m   E 210-230
 1200 m   S 210-          (deliberately malformed)
 1400 m   S 210-230
 1600 m   Up -3%
 1800 m   E 210-230

The grade labels deliberately exercise shift parsing, not terrain modeling.
Both files use the existing fit-tool dependency and the planner course exporter.
Course playback is distinct from activity FIT sensor playback; see
datafield/BUILD.txt for simulator limitations and the manual checklist.
